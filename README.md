### 📊 Operations Performance & Decision Support

**OpsIntel --- Operations Intelligence Workbench**

An interactive operations analytics workbench for reviewing performance
across locations, services, and reporting periods. It supports data
preparation, KPI monitoring, data-quality checks, structured
investigation, and analyst-reviewed management reporting.

**Key capabilities:**

-   Executive KPIs, interactive filters, and operational performance
    trends
-   Baseline-versus-current period comparisons, with coverage warnings
    and optional per-day normalization
-   Validated column mapping, data-quality diagnostics, preview-first
    cleanup, and original-data restoration
-   Configurable KPI targets with warning, critical, and unavailable
    states
-   Saved projects and executive PDF, CSV, and JSON exports

**Transferable skills demonstrated:** KPI governance · Business analysis
· Data validation · Descriptive analytics · Structured investigation ·
Executive reporting

**Technology:** `Python` · `Streamlit` · `Pandas` · `NumPy` · `Plotly` ·
`SQLite` · `Matplotlib` · `ReportLab`

**Airline Ground Turnaround Performance Case Study**

An interactive operational dashboard for reviewing turnaround variance,
assumed SLA compliance, and on-time performance. It compares results
across stations and ground handlers and supports filtering by station,
handler, and scheduled time band.

**Key indicators from the simulated dataset (January--March 2026):**

-   Flight-turnaround records analysed: **1,500**
-   SLA breach rate: **approximately 45.7%**
-   On-time performance (OTP): **approximately 74.7%**
-   Average turnaround variance: **+10.1 minutes**

These figures are calculated from simulated portfolio data and are not
verified results from a real airline operation.

**Transferable skills demonstrated:** Service-level monitoring · Vendor
performance review · Process variation analysis · Exception
identification · Operational KPI reporting

**Technology:** `Python` · `Streamlit` · `Pandas` · `Plotly` · `CSV`
